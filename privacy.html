Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 15, 2026

Wasi's Discoveries is a non-commercial, educational portfolio dedicated to the principles of privacy, transparency, and open science. Wasi believe that education should be accessible to all.

Our Core Philosophy: We do not track you. We do not sell your data. We do not use third-party analytics. Your privacy is respected by design.

1. What We DO NOT Collect

To ensure your privacy, this website explicitly does not collect, store, or transmit the following:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.
  • No Analytics: We do not use any Analytics or any other tracking service. We do not know who visits this site, where they are from, or how long they stay.
  • No Cookies: We do not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies.
  • No Server-Side Logs: Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may generate standard server logs for security, but these are not accessed, analyzed, or shared by us for user profiling.
  • No Third-Party Scripts: The site contains no external scripts that could leak your data to other companies.

2. What We DO Store (Locally Only)

The only data stored on your device is strictly necessary to provide a personalized user experience. This data is saved in your browser's LocalStorage and never leaves your device.

Data Stored Locally:

  • Theme Preference: Whether you prefer "Light" or "Dark" mode.
  • Accent Color: Your chosen color scheme (Blue, Green, Red, or Purple).
  • Quick Notes: Any temporary notes you save using the "Quick Note" feature (if enabled).
  • Service Worker Cache: To enable offline access, the site caches its own files (HTML, CSS, JS) on your device. This is standard for Progressive Web Apps (PWAs) and contains no personal data.

Note: If you clear your browser cache, this data will be deleted.

3. How We Use Your Data

Since we do not collect personal data, we do not use it for:

  • Marketing or advertising.
  • Selling to third parties.
  • Building user profiles.
  • Personalized content recommendations based on behavior.

The only purpose of local storage is to remember your preferences so you don't have to re-select them every time you visit.

4. External Links

This website may contain links to external educational resources, scientific papers, or other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

This site is an educational resource intended for students and teachers. Since we do not collect any personal data, we do not require parental consent, nor do we knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the site constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the technical implementation of our privacy measures, please contact us via the "About" section of this website.
